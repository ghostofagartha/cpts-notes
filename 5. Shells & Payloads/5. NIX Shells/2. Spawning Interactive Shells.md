**Interactive**
- `/bin/sh -i`

### Perl
**Perl to Shell**
- `perl -e 'exec "/bin/sh";'`
- `perl: exec "/bin/sh"`

****
### Ruby
**Ruby to Shell**
- `ruby: exec "/bin/sh"`

****
### Lua
**Lua to Shell**
- `lua: os.execute('/bin/sh')`

****
### AWK
**AWK to Shell**
- `awk 'BEGIN {system("/bin/sh")}'`

****
### Find
**Using Fine For A Shell**
- `find / -name nameoffile -exec /bin/awk 'BEGIN {system("/bin/sh")}' \;`

****
### Using Exec To Launch A Shell
- `find . -exec /bin/bash \; -quit`

****
