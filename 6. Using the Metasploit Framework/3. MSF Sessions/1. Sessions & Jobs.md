### Using Sessions
**Sending Session to Background**
- `background` or [CTRL] + [Z] in meterpreter
**Listing Active Sessions**
- `sessions`
**Interacting with a Session**
- `sessions -i [session number]`

****
### Jobs
**Running an Exploit as a Background Job**
- `exploit -j`
**Listing Running Jobs**
- `jobs -l`
